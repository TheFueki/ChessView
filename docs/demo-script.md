# ChessView Demo Script

Target length: 2 to 3 minutes

## Goal

Show that ChessView is a complete chess product, not just a board demo.

## Suggested Flow

1. Open the public landing page.
2. Log in and arrive at the dashboard.
3. Open two browser sessions and join the lobby.
4. Start a live game and make a few moves.
5. Show premove or move responsiveness briefly.
6. Finish the game.
7. Open history and jump into replay.
8. Step through replay and show live Stockfish guidance.
9. Open the analysis board.
10. Show board editor.
11. Paste a PGN and load it.
12. Open puzzle mode and solve or retry a puzzle.
13. Open tournaments and show standings/pairings.

## Talking Points

- live play is server-authoritative
- replay and study stay in sync with the displayed position
- puzzles and tournaments sit inside the same product shell
- the repo supports both Docker and local workflows

## Recording Tips

- prepare two test accounts ahead of time
- keep the time control short for the demo
- have a finished game ready in history as a backup
- avoid over-explaining implementation details during the main walkthrough
